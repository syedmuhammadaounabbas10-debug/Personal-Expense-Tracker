# Personal Expense Tracker (Personal Paisa Manager)

A modern Android personal finance and expense tracking application built with **Kotlin**, **Jetpack Compose**, **Room Database**, **Firebase Auth**, and **Material Design 3**.

---

## 🎙️ Voice Entry (Urdu & English)

Personal Expense Tracker features **Voice-Based Expense Entry** supporting Urdu, Roman Urdu, and English speech recognition.

### How to Use:
1. Tap the **Mic Floating Action Button** on the Dashboard or Transaction History screens, or tap the **Mic icon** next to the amount field on the Add/Edit Transaction screen.
2. Speak your expense or income sentence naturally in **Urdu**, **Roman Urdu**, or **English**.
3. Review the extracted details on the **Confirmation Sheet** (Amount, Category, Type, Date, Note) and tap **Save** or **Edit in full form**.

### Example Supported Phrases:
- `"Dost ke sath dinner par 1500 rupay lag gaye"` → **Expense**, Rs 1,500, **Food & Dining**
- `"Petrol dalwaya 2000 ka"` → **Expense**, Rs 2,000, **Transport**
- `"Bijli ka bill 8500 diya"` → **Expense**, Rs 8,500, **Bills & Utilities**
- `"Dedh hazar ka rashan liya"` → **Expense**, Rs 1,500, **Groceries**
- `"Chai pe 150 kharch hue"` → **Expense**, Rs 150, **Food & Dining**
- `"Salary 50000 mili"` → **Income**, Rs 50,000, **Salary**
- `"دو ہزار کا پیٹرول ڈلوایا"` → **Expense**, Rs 2,000, **Transport**
- `"Kal 500 ka petrol dalwaya"` → **Expense**, Rs 500, **Transport**, dated yesterday
- `"Spent 300 on rickshaw today"` → **Expense**, Rs 300, **Transport**
